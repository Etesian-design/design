# Principles

1. **The content is the interface.** (N9) Minimal chrome. No frames around content, no decorative
   gradients except the toolbar. Lists, photos and text fill the screen edge to edge.
2. **Swipe, don't press.** (N9) The N9 had no Home button. Navigation between apps and Home is a
   swipe from a screen edge. Inside apps, the Back button is always bottom-left in the toolbar.
3. **Calm, light typography.** (N9) Light weight is the default; bold is reserved for buttons,
   page titles and emphasis. Type is large (default 24 n9px) and set with generous whitespace.
4. **High contrast, few colours.** (N9) Near-black on light grey (`blanco`) or white on pure black
   (inverted). Colour is used for one thing per screen: the app's **accent**.
5. **One accent per app.** (N9) Each app owns one colour (Phone green, Email blue, Music magenta...).
   It tints the header, selected states and primary buttons. Nothing else is coloured.
6. **Squircles for things you can hold.** (N9/canon) Every thumb-sized object (app icon, avatar,
   contact photo, thumbnail, file icon, loading ring) is a superellipse squircle; never a circle,
   never a rounded rectangle.
7. **No shadows, except overlays.** (N9/canon) Surfaces are flat. Only things floating above the
   page (dialogs, menus, banners, toasts) get a single soft shadow.
8. **One-handed.** (N9) Primary actions live at the bottom, within thumb reach.
9. **Motion explains space.** (N9) Pages slide horizontally (drill-down), sheets slide up,
   dialogs fade/scale, banners drop from the top. Motion is quick and never decorative.
10. **Respect the person.** (EXT) Everything works with a keyboard, a screen reader, at 200% zoom,
    with reduced motion and in both themes.
