# N9 and the port

Etesian is the N9 on an Android phone. Where it looks or works differently from the N9, it is on purpose: Android
needs it, the hardware differs, or the N9 had no answer. Every difference has a setting; the default is the N9
wherever the N9 had one, and every addition is a switch that starts **off** (a fresh phone is the pure N9).
The settings named here are drawn in **OS / Etesian settings**.

## Additions the N9 never had (all off by default)

Single hand mode (Gestures → Single hand mode, size 50 to 90%), Breeze edge bar or floating button (Breeze),
the Standby screen editor (Lock screen → Standby screen), glance line and weather under the clock, emergency
information on the lock screen, inline reply, actions and snooze, live updates, priority people, ghost rows for
dismissed notifications, Today in Events, the "Copied" bubble, the N9 share sheet and suggested people, sharing a
Wi-Fi network by QR code, private space, privacy indicators, pull down to search and search everything.
Each is mocked in **Phone / Android features** or the **OS** pages.

## Hardware

The phone (F(x)tec Pro1-X) has a slide-out keyboard and a curved screen edge. With the keyboard out, only the white
keyboard strip shows on screen; still fingers within the curved grip zone are ignored by the touch firmware, so edge
controls (Breeze handle, edge swipes) start a little further in than on the N9.
