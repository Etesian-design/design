## Android features, the Etesian way

Today's Android has features the N9 never had. Each row is one of them, with Etesian's answer as a mock (under *Phone / Android features*) and where it stands: **Built** (on the phone, with its device test), **Built, changed** (built differently from the mock), **Partly built**, **Not built yet** or **Declined**. Every addition is off by default.

### Not built yet

- **Dictionary**: the N9 word tracker over the word being typed, its candidate list, and suggestions in the strip with a hardware keyboard.
- **Lock-screen music**: waits for the lock screen redesign.
- **Reply with a hardware keyboard**: the white strip on its own when the keyboard is out.
- **In-app back animation**: the toolbar Back already reaches apps as Back; their own back animation is still to come.
